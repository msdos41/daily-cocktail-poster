# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Just One Sip is for cocktail-curious casual drinkers who want a polished daily idea without opening a recipe database. They may be browsing in the evening, looking for a drink to make, saving a visual, or sharing a concise recommendation with someone else.

The audience values mood first and clarity second: a memorable poster should draw them in, then the recipe should be close, simple, and trustworthy.

## Product Purpose

Just One Sip presents one cocktail, one visual, and one concise recipe every day. The site exists to make cocktail discovery feel like encountering a new poster, not searching a catalog.

Success means the first viewport feels immersive and specific, the daily recommendation is obvious, the recipe is easy to reach, and the archive remains a curated set of past picks rather than an exhaustive database.

## Brand Personality

Midnight Pour: cinematic, refined, concise.

The voice is night-bar editorial: image-led, warm, compact, and atmospheric. It should feel like a drink has been staged under low bar light, with just enough words to make the choice feel intentional.

## Anti-references

Avoid AI-template cliches: generic hero/card-grid patterns, purple gradients, decorative glassmorphism, feature-marketing copy, repeated icon-card sections, and empty visual decoration.

Avoid making the site feel like a sterile recipe app, a SaaS landing page, or a faux speakeasy theme. The poster experience leads; utility supports it.

## Design Principles

1. Lead with the poster. The daily visual is the primary impression; text and controls should protect it, not compete with it.
2. One drink, little friction. Every screen should make the current cocktail, recipe access, sharing, and archive navigation feel immediate.
3. Mood with discipline. Darkness, blur, shadow, and warm accents are allowed when they improve legibility over imagery. Decorative effects are not the brand.
4. Multilingual by default. English and Simplified Chinese content must remain readable, balanced, and free of mobile overflow.
5. Static and durable. Design choices must fit the static Astro MVP and should not require SSR, APIs, CMS, or scheduled rebuilds.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG AA contrast for text and controls wherever possible, especially over imagery. Preserve semantic navigation, keyboard-reachable actions, visible focus, and clear accessible labels for icon controls.

Respect reduced-motion preferences for any future choreography. Maintain the existing title-fitting discipline: CJK and single-word titles prefer one line, multi-word titles wrap only at natural breaks, and no mobile layout should create horizontal overflow.
